Account & Data Deletion
Request permanent deletion of your Collection Book account and associated data.
Delete your account from the app

Collection Book allows you to request account deletion directly from within the app.

Open Collection Book and go to Settings → Delete Account and follow the instructions shown on the screen.

Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have access to the Collection Book app, you can request deletion of your account by contacting us.

Send an email with the subject:

Collection Book Account Deletion Request

Please include the mobile number registered with your Collection Book account so that we can identify your account.

Request Account Deletion
Verification

To protect your account from unauthorized deletion, we may ask you to verify ownership of the registered mobile number or provide additional information necessary to identify your account.

What data will be deleted?

When an eligible account deletion request is completed, data associated with your Collection Book account will be permanently deleted, including, where applicable:

  • Your Collection Book account and profile information
  • Your business profile information
  • Customer records belonging to your business
  • Ledger entries belonging to your business
  • Employee configuration belonging to your business
  • Notifications associated with your account
Employee accounts

If you use Collection Book as an employee of another business, deleting your account does not automatically delete business records that belong to that business.

Records created while acting on behalf of a business may remain part of that business's records.

Important

Account deletion is permanent. Once your account and eligible associated data have been deleted, they may not be recoverable.
